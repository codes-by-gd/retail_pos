# Expenses

**Status:** ⏳ planned (Phase 6)
**Purpose:** Record shop running costs so profit and cash reports are correct.

## Features
- Expense categories (rent, salary, electricity, transport…)
- Expense entry: date, branch, category, amount, payment method, note, optional GST input & bill photo
- Paid from register cash (shows in Z-report) or from bank
- Reports by category and period

## Permissions
`expenses.manage`.
